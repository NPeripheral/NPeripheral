## Your business doesn't need to be everywhere

It needs to appear where your audience is.

Most businesses don't have a visibility problem so much as a consistency
problem. Being present on five platforms badly is worse than being present on
one deliberately. We help you pick the ground worth holding — then help you
hold it.

## What we do

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Social Media Management** | Your accounts stay active and consistent without you running them |
| **Content Strategy** | A plan for what to post, where, and why |
| **Content Creation** | Graphics, captions and assets built for the feed they live in |
| **Short-Form Video** | Concepts and structure for TikTok, Reels and Shorts |
| **Social Media Optimization** | Profiles that explain the business at a glance |
| **Community Engagement** | Staying responsive to the people who reach out |
| **Social Media Strategy** | A plan built around your goals and your resources |

Services are chosen per client. No engagement includes all seven by default.

## How we work

**Discover** → **Strategize** → **Create** → **Appear**

We look at what you have, write a plan you approve before anything ships,
produce the content, and keep it running consistently — adjusting as we learn
what your audience responds to.

## Pricing

Quoted per business. Platforms, posting frequency, content requirements and
level of management vary enormously, and a fixed rate card would be wrong for
almost everyone. Tell us what you need and you'll have a scope and a price,
usually within one business day.

## What we won't promise

Follower counts, engagement rates or revenue figures. Those depend on your
offer and your market as much as on marketing, and anyone guaranteeing them is
selling something else. What we commit to is the work: a strategy built for
your business, content produced on schedule, and honest reporting on what is
and isn't landing.

This repo holds the source for [nperipheral.com](https://www.nperipheral.com) —
a Next.js 16 site using the App Router, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer
Motion and Lenis. No stock photography and no image assets: every visual is
drawn at runtime.

```bash
npm install
cp .env.example .env.local
npm run dev
```

Full setup, environment variables, project structure and content conventions
are in **[docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md)**.
